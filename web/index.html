      REPORT THE QUANTITY THE EXPERIMENT SUPPORTS

      “Not estimable” is a result, not a software failure.

      A relative IC50 is halfway between the fitted asymptotes. A control-defined IC50 is the concentration at a predeclared 50% response level. These need not coincide, and the second may not exist even when the first is well resolved. GraphPad’s definition guide ↗

      LOCALIZED TRANSITION

      Report an estimate, with its conditions.

      Declare the endpoint, controls, units, exposure, model and constraints. Show observations, uncertainty and the attainable effect. An observed threshold bracket is not itself a confidence interval. Assay Guidance Manual ↗

      OUTSIDE THE TESTED RANGE

      Separate a bound from no finite crossing.

      “50% inhibition not observed through 10 µM” is weaker and often safer than claiming a finite IC50 above 10 µM. A justified > or < bound must state its assumptions; a partial-effect plateau may never cross the reference. Relative and absolute thresholds ↗

      WRONG RESPONSE SHAPE

      Do not force one sigmoid.

      Rebounds, biphasic responses and strong residual structure challenge a monotone 4PL. More flexible models can be useful, but introduce additional parameters and new identifiability demands. Ritz et al., 2015 ↗

      GOOD FIT, WRONG BIOLOGY

      Report the measured response, not a mechanism.

      Aggregation, reporter interference, depletion and incubation history can produce convincing concentration responses. IC50 is assay-dependent and is not automatically Ki, affinity or evidence of target engagement. Tonge, 2019 ↗

      What this prototype intentionally refuses to decide

      The flags are conservative, declared teaching heuristics, not universal pharmacological or regulatory criteria. A model with no flag is not validated, and a flag does not prove an assay is unusable. Formal inference requires an assay-specific analysis plan and an appropriate sampling and error model.

      For repeated experiments, keep run identity and estimate between-run variability. For dividing cells, consider growth-rate-aware endpoints rather than interpreting endpoint viability as a direct measure of death. Tansey et al. ↗ · Hafner et al., 2016 ↗

      INSPECTABLE MATHEMATICS / DECLARED LIMITATIONS

      What the tool actually computes

      y(x) = B + (T − B) / [1 + (x / 10ᵐ)ʰ]

      The decreasing 4PL uses bottom B, top T, log10 relative midpoint m, and positive Hill slope h. At zero concentration the limit is T; zero is never replaced by a tiny artificial dose. Wells are averaged within concentration, then each concentration mean receives equal weight.

      The conditional bottom/top problem is solved by bounded linear least squares. A coarse midpoint/slope search is refined numerically. Numerical domains: −50 ≤ B ≤ T ≤ 150%, 0.1 ≤ h ≤ 5, and m within four log units beyond the tested positive concentration range. These are computational restrictions, not learned biological facts.

      LR(m) = n ln[SSE(m) / SSEmin] ≤ 3.84146

      With independent Gaussian concentration means of common unknown variance, the profile likelihood reoptimizes the other parameters at fixed m. The cutoff is an asymptotic, one-parameter likelihood-ratio approximation. Small samples, boundaries, heteroscedasticity, shared normalization and model mismatch can invalidate nominal coverage; this app does not calibrate that coverage. Profile-likelihood methodology ↗

      The control-defined threshold is solved separately as x₅₀ = 10ᵐ[(T−50)/(50−B)]¹/ʰ, only if B < 50 < T. Its preferred displayed value is a local linear interpolation in log concentration between adjacent measured means bracketing 50%. That tested-dose bracket is not a confidence interval. No absolute-IC50 confidence interval is computed.

      Audit rules, version 0.4.0

      Numerical localization requires at least seven positive doses, one descending crossing of the fitted midpoint response, an in-range midpoint, no parameter-bound hit, and a finite connected relative profile no wider than two log units. The observed response span must exceed max(10 percentage points, 3 × estimated mean-level noise). An upward reversal larger than max(8, 3 × noise), or mean RMSE greater than max(8, 2 × noise), triggers review.

      Mean-level noise is the RMS of within-dose SD/√replicate count across replicated concentrations. It is descriptive and assumes wells are independently noisy; it does not measure between-run reproducibility. Numerical rules are original teaching choices and are not recommendations attributed to a guideline.

      Evidence behind the questions

      ICH Q2(R2) and ICH Q14 frame intended-purpose analytical performance and development; their example criteria are not universal discovery IC50 rules. Sebaugh (2011) recommends at least two concentrations beyond each bend for relative midpoints and at least two predicted responses on each side of 50% for absolute estimates. The prototype does not implement or certify those full recommendations. Its seven-dose and other numerical rules remain original teaching heuristics, not published acceptance standards.

      The USP <1034> preview is not a verified copy of the complete current chapter. We do not claim USP compliance. The 2024 revision preview is labeled a proposal; current official status must be checked in USP-NF.

      USE THE RIGHT PART OF THE SERIES

      This installment reconstructs the source-to-claim chain.

      It does not replace the earlier fitting, censoring, comparability or mechanism tools. The overlap review is explicit: inherited ideas stay attributed, and specialist questions go back to their existing workbenches.

      Is the midpoint located?

      Convergence Is Not Measurement / censored-potency already addresses observed bracketing, free versus constrained fits and unresolved potency reporting. Curve Check retains a browser diagnostic, not a new claim to that idea.

      Were bounds lost from the table?

      Censor Check / No. 06 audits reported values, relation signs, filtering and within-paper comparisons. It does not reconstruct the raw curve that produced each record.

      Can these lots or methods be compared?

      Comparability Check / No. 07 owns the full lot/method claim audit, crossed bridges, normalization walkthrough and nonparallel comparisons. The dose-ratio panel here is a small teaching bridge.

      Which signaling experiment separates explanations?

      Crosstalk Check / No. 08 models stimulation history, interventions and reporter dynamics. The identical-sigmoid example here demonstrates a limit of inference, not a replacement signaling simulator.

      Did processing alter the image-derived result?

      The Threshold Is Part of the Measurement already examines thresholding, detector effects, segmentation and sampling. This installment applies an explicit raw-control reconstruction to concentration-response records.

      Is exposure or assay state changing?

      Use Free-Dose Check, Washout Check, Protein-Clock Check, or Encounter Check for the corresponding mechanistic design problem, rather than inferring it from an IC50 shape.
